# Deleting the Markdown Files

Yes, **you can absolutely delete them manually!** 

These `.md` files are just temporary text files I created specifically for you to read. They are not connected to your app's actual code. 

Deleting them will not break anything, and it will not mess anything up for me. Feel free to just right-click them in your editor and hit **Delete** whenever you are done reading them to keep your workspace clean!
