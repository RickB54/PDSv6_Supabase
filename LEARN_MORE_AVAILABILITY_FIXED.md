# ✅ LEARN MORE MODAL - AVAILABILITY FIXED

## 🎯 Issue Fixed

**Problem:** Blocked days not showing in "Learn More" modal calendar  
**Cause:** AvailabilityPicker was only checking manual blocks  
**Solution:** Updated to use hybrid availability system

---

## ✅ What Now Shows

### In "Learn More" Modal Calendar

**Before:**
- Only showed existing bookings
- Manual blocks not visible
- Google Calendar blocks not visible

**After:**
- ✅ **Manual blocks** (from Availability Manager)
- ✅ **Google Calendar blocks** (personal appointments)
- ✅ **Existing bookings** (customer appointments)
- ✅ **Blue dots** on blocked days
- ✅ **Accurate time slots**

---

## 🎨 How It Works

### Customer Opens "Learn More"
1. Clicks "Learn More" on any service
2. Sees service details
3. Scrolls to "Check Availability"
4. Sees calendar with **blue dots**

### Blue Dots Indicate:
- Days you manually blocked
- Days with Google Calendar events
- Days with existing bookings
- **Any day with limited/no availability**

### Clicking a Date:
- Shows **only available time slots**
- Blocked times are hidden
- Customer sees: "9:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM"
- (Your blocked 10:00 AM doesn't show)

---

## 📊 Example

### Your Blocks:
- **Manual:** Jan 15 (full day - vacation)
- **Google:** Jan 16 at 2 PM (dentist)
- **Booking:** Jan 17 at 10 AM (John Smith)

### Customer Sees in Modal:
**Calendar:**
- Jan 15: **Blue dot** (no time slots available)
- Jan 16: **Blue dot** (2 PM unavailable)
- Jan 17: **Blue dot** (10 AM unavailable)

**Clicks Jan 16:**
- Available: 9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 1:00 PM, 3:00 PM, 4:00 PM
- **Not shown:** 2:00 PM (your dentist appointment)

**Clicks Jan 15:**
- Message: "No availability on January 15, 2026. Please select another date."
