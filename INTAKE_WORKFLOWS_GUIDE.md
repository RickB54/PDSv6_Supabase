# Intake & Interaction Workflows

The Golden Rule of Data Linking: To prevent duplicates and keep customer histories complete, you must always follow the correct intake workflow based on how the customer contacted you.

---

## 1. Online Booking — What happens automatically

**Confirmed safe.** The online booking page shares the exact same database save function as the admin backend — same 3-tier match, same linking logic.

When a customer books online, the system runs this cascade automatically:
1. **Email match**
2. **Name match**
3. **Phone match** (strips formatting)

### Your only action after an online booking arrives

1. **Confirm it appears in your calendar correctly**
   Check service, date, vehicle. If the customer is recognized by any of the 3 fields, nothing else is needed. (Auto-linked)

2. **Open their customer card — check Booking Lifecycle panel**
   If the Booking Lifecycle panel on the right shows the job, the link is confirmed. If it still says "No Booking Data Yet", the booking landed on a duplicate — see fix below.

3. **Optional — Log Activity to note it came in online**
   "Online booking received — full interior, Thursday 9am." Keeps your interaction history complete.

### If Booking Lifecycle still says "No Booking Data Yet" — duplicate fix
This only happens if the customer used a completely different email, name, AND phone than what's on file.

**Fix:** Open the booking → Edit → Select Customer dropdown → choose their correct master profile → save. Then go to Customer Profiles and delete the duplicate Prospect record.

> **The rule:** Do nothing — system handles it. Just verify Booking Lifecycle panel shows the job on their card. Log Activity if you want a record of it.

## 2. Direct / Admin booking — Recommended flow

1. **Always start in Customer Profiles — search first**
   Even if you're certain who it is. This one habit eliminates all duplicate and linking problems.

2. **Open their card — tap the green "Book Job" button**
   Never navigate to the Bookings page directly for a known customer. Always use Book Job from their card.

3. **Fill in all job details — save the booking**
   Service, date, vehicle, price. Add a booking note if anything unusual applies to this job. Check that the Booking Lifecycle panel on the right updates — this is your confirmation the link is solid.

4. **Back to their card — Log Activity with how it came in**
   "Walk-in", "Repeat customer texted me", "Outbound call — booked full detail Friday." One line.

> **The rule:** Customer Profiles (search) → Book Job button → save → confirm Booking Lifecycle panel → Log Activity.
> *Never start a booking from the Admin Bookings page for a known customer.*

## 3. Website / Email inquiry — Recommended flow

1. **Read the inquiry fully before doing anything**
   Note their name, vehicle, service interest, date preference, and contact info. Keep the email open.

2. **Search Customer Profiles — check for existing record**
   Search by name AND email. Returning customers sometimes use the contact form with a different email than on file.

3. **Open card (or create new) — tap Log Activity**
   "Inbound email — wants full detail, prefers Thursday." Creates a lead trail before any booking exists.

4. **Reply via Engagement Hub — offer 1–2 time slots**
   Engagement Hub logs your reply automatically in the Correspondences tab so it's part of their history.

5. **Once they confirm — tap "Book Job" from their card**
   Always use the green Book Job button. Never go to the Bookings page directly for a customer already in the system.

6. **Log Activity again — "Confirmed, booked for [date]"**
   Full trail: inquiry → your reply → confirmation → booking. All on their record in All Data.

> **The rule:** Search → Log Activity (inquiry) → Engagement Hub (reply) → Book Job button (on confirm) → Log Activity (confirmation).

---

## 4. Phone call — Recommended flow

1. **Go to Customer Profiles — search first**
   Search by name, phone, or email before anything else. Even on a live call. This is what prevents duplicates.

2. **Select existing — or create new**
   If found, open their card. If not, click "+ Add" to create their profile first and save it.

3. **Tap "Log Activity" — record the call**
   Quick note: "Inbound — wants full interior Thursday." One line is enough. This goes to the Activities tab.

4. **Tap the green "Book Job" button on their card**
   Booking from their card pre-fills their exact database ID — the booking links to them perfectly, guaranteed every time.

5. **Fill in job details and save**
   Date, service, vehicle, price. Add a booking note for anything job-specific. Verify Booking Lifecycle panel on the right shows the job — that confirms the link.

> **The rule:** Search → Select/Create Profile → Log Activity → Book Job button → Save.
