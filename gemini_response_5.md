# How the Bookings Badge Currently Works

Right now, that blue badge (with the "1") acts as an **"Upcoming Active Jobs" counter**, rather than an "Action Required" notification. 

Currently, it adds up any booking in your system that is:
* Pending
* Tentative
* **Confirmed**
* In Progress

Because your booking is currently marked as **Confirmed**, the system says "Hey, you have 1 active job on your plate!" and keeps the badge there until the job is marked as "Completed" or "Archived".
