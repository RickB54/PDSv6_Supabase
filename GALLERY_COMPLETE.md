## 📸 To Add Your First Photo:

1. Click **"Upload Media"** (green button, top right)
2. Select customer: e.g., "Linda Thompson"
3. Select their vehicle: e.g., "2020 Toyota Camry"
4. Choose category: "General" (or Before/After)
5. Click "Upload" or "Camera" to select photo
6. Click "Save Media"
7. **Boom!** Photo appears in gallery with Linda's name tagged!
